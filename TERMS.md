Last updated: October 1, 2026.

"Build, Ship, Shape" Developer Hackathon 2026 (Alexa+ track). It is
provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.

- You run the server yourself; you are responsible for the machine,

  medical advice. Consult a professional for training or diet guidance.
- The developer may change or discontinue this hackathon project at any
  time.

Open an issue at https://github.com/hahahahahahahahah6/fitlog-mcp/issues
